Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Radwan Labs (1000819600 Ontario Ltd.), Toronto, Ontario · hello@radwanlabs.com

What we collect

When you scan a site, we receive the web address you enter and fetch that site's public pages. We don't ask for your name, email or account. Our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, time of request) for security and abuse prevention.

How we use it

To run the scan and show you the result, to block abuse (rate limiting by IP address), and to keep the service working. Scan results are cached in memory for up to one hour so repeat scans are fast, then discarded.

Cookies and tracking

FooterCheck uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Emailing us

If you click "Fix it for me", your own email app opens a message to hello@radwanlabs.com. If you send it, we use your email and the report only to reply and do the work you ask for. We never sell or share it.

Sharing

We don't sell or share personal information. Our hosting provider processes server logs on our behalf.

Retention

Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. Emails are kept as long as needed to serve you, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@radwanlabs.com. We reply within 30 days.

Children

FooterCheck is a business tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes
